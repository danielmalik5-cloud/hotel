# The Token Hotel – Terms of Service

Last updated: November 15, 2026

## The service

The Token Hotel Uploader is an internal tool that publishes episodes of The Token Hotel, a fictional AI-made comedy series, to our own YouTube channel (@TheTokenHotel). It is not offered to the public and has no users other than the channel owner.

## YouTube API Services

This tool uses YouTube API Services. Its use is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service (https://www.youtube.com/t/terms) and the Google Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy). We follow the YouTube API Services Terms of Service and Developer Policies.

## Content

All episodes are fictional. Characters and voices are AI-generated, and every upload is labeled as altered or synthetic content. Episodes are family friendly and do not depict real people, brands or politics.

## Data

The tool does not collect any personal data. See our Privacy Policy: https://github.com/danielmalik5-cloud/hotel/blob/main/PRIVACY.md

## Access and revocation

The channel owner can revoke the tool's access to the YouTube account at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
